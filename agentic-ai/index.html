Meet Bradley.

BIG AV’s agentic AI technology connects system control, project information and support. Tell it what needs to happen, and it coordinates the steps using the systems and permissions available to it.

Control. Create. Support.

We use Bradley in our own operations and build it into the environments we deliver.

Control the environment

Operate rooms, lighting, audio, video and conferencing through natural language, scenes and schedules. Routine actions can run automatically once the client has authorized them.

Create project documents

Turn specifications, drawings and project records into bills of material, labour breakdowns, cable schedules and operator interfaces. Technical staff review the outputs before they are issued or used on a project.

Keep support moving

Detect system faults, collect logs and configuration details, and open support cases with the information needed to act. Follow-up and documented fixes become part of the system record.

From fault to follow-up

Less time gathering information.
More time solving the problem.

A useful support case starts before anyone picks up the phone. Bradley brings the evidence and the history together.

  1. Detect and document

    Identify a fault in a connected system and collect the relevant logs, configuration and recent changes.

  2. Reach the right team

    Open and follow a case through the support channels available for that system, including manufacturer support where connected.

  3. Record the result

    Document the response and correction. Recurring faults can use correction sequences that a qualified person has reviewed and authorized.

Deployment and control

Your systems.
Clear permissions.

Bradley can run on premises or in a private cloud, with connections to your enterprise systems. We define the hosting, system access and data requirements with your team before deployment.

People authorize routine actions in advance. Changes to design, pricing, firmware, safety, privacy or security require individual review. An action outside the agreed permissions goes to a person.

Activity and support records capture what happened and what changed, giving operators and technical staff a shared history to work from.
