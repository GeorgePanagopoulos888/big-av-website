AV integration,

BIG AV is a Toronto-based AV and control systems integrator and a division of Salex Inc. We bring the disciplines behind a complete environment together, from design and construction through operation and support.

A complete integration business.

Our work spans audio, video, lighting control, conferencing, networking, automation and the connections between them. We coordinate designers, technical specialists, installers and project partners around the finished environment.

Clients can engage BIG AV for a complete project or for specialist design, programming, commissioning and support. In either case, the work has a clear point of responsibility.

Our foundation

Part of Salex.

Founded in 1973, Salex is an Ontario lighting agency with experience in specification, product selection and project delivery.

BIG AV extends that expertise into integrated AV and control systems. The relationship brings lighting knowledge and systems integration together for clients, designers and project teams.

Leadership

Built on experience.

Founded by George Panagopoulos, BIG AV draws on more than 25 years of experience building AV businesses and delivering complex integrated systems.

That experience spans thousands of installations across immersive attractions, major venues, enterprise facilities and connected buildings, alongside the development of technology products and service operations that support them.

Developing what comes next.

We also develop Bradley, our agentic AI technology for system control, engineering documentation and support. It supports our own operations and brings those capabilities into the environments we deliver.
