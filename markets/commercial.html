Our markets

Commercial

Connected workplaces, from the meeting room to the entire building.

The environment

A complete workplace, planned together.

BIG AV designs and delivers AV and control systems for corporate offices, boardrooms, collaboration spaces and divisible meeting rooms. We bring conferencing, shared media, room controls and building connections into one coordinated project.

The planning starts with how people work: who joins remotely, how rooms change throughout the day and what the internal support team needs. Those decisions shape the infrastructure, equipment and controls across the workplace.

Systems

Across rooms, floors and facilities.

Meeting and divisible roomsConferencing, cameras, microphones and displays for local and remote participants. Audio and controls follow the configuration when spaces divide or combine.
Building-wide mediaDigital signage, paging and networked audio and video distribution across shared spaces, floors and facilities.
Room and building controlTouch panels, scheduling and lighting control, with connections to building systems developed alongside the facilities team.
Consistent workplace standardsRepeatable designs and interfaces help staff use different rooms and help IT support multiple locations, with documentation and training to match.

Project delivery

Ready for the working day.

We coordinate cabling, networks, racks and power with the wider project team, then install, program and test the systems against everyday use. Documentation and staff training prepare the workplace for handover.

For occupied offices, we can phase installation and upgrades around access, working hours and the rooms that need to stay in service. Monitoring, troubleshooting and planned updates support the workplace after opening.
