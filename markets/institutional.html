Our markets

Institutional

AV infrastructure for learning, public presentation and shared facilities.

The environment

Many users. A coherent system.

BIG AV designs and delivers AV systems for classrooms, lecture spaces, auditoria, assembly areas and meeting facilities. We bring presentation, sound, conferencing, information displays and control into a coordinated plan for a building or campus.

In spaces used by different departments and visiting presenters, consistent operation matters. We develop the room requirements with users and support staff, then document the systems for procurement, construction and ongoing operation.

Systems

From daily teaching to major assemblies.

Learning and collaborationDisplays, projection, microphones, recording and conferencing for teaching and meetings, with consistent controls for local and remote participation.
Auditoria and assembly spacesSpeech reinforcement, presentation systems, lighting control and operator interfaces for larger gatherings.
Campus informationDigital signage, paging and media distribution for shared spaces and public communications across buildings.
Design and tender documentsCoordinated drawings, equipment schedules, system descriptions and technical review support for procurement, followed by records for the completed installation.

Project delivery

Designed for construction and continued use.

We coordinate the AV design with the project consultants and trades, carry out installation and programming, and test the completed systems with the people who will operate them. Room standards and system records give support teams a consistent starting point.

For renovations, we can phase the work around teaching, events and facility access, integrating new systems with equipment that remains in use. Training, troubleshooting and replacement planning support the facilities over time.
