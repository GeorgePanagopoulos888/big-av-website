Our markets

Healthcare

Clear communication for consultation, training and the daily work of a healthcare facility.

The environment

Connected teams across the facility.

BIG AV designs and delivers AV systems for healthcare meeting rooms, consultation spaces, staff training and public communications. We connect conferencing, presentation, sound and room controls across new facilities and renovations.

We work with the clinical, administrative, facilities and IT teams to understand room use, network requirements and access to equipment. The design gives each group the tools they need and gives the support team a clear record of how the systems work.

Systems

Communication where it is needed.

Consultation and meeting roomsConferencing, cameras, microphones and displays for healthcare and administrative teams, with consistent controls across rooms.
Staff trainingAudio reinforcement, presentation, recording and flexible room configurations for instruction and professional development.
Facility informationDigital signage, paging and media distribution for staff communications and public areas across the facility.
Room controlsInterfaces that bring audio, video and lighting controls together, with operation developed around the staff using each space.

Project delivery

Planned around an operating facility.

Infrastructure and equipment locations are coordinated with the construction and IT teams. We plan installation access, test conferencing and presentation workflows, and provide documentation and training before handover.

In occupied facilities, the sequence of work matters. We plan replacements and changeovers with the facility team, accounting for the spaces that must remain available and the access needed for future service. Ongoing support includes troubleshooting and planned system upgrades.
