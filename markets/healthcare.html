We design healthcare AV and control around dependable communication, practical operation, and long-term support.

SECTION 1 | THE ENVIRONMENTS

Communication without distraction.

Healthcare environments can combine clinical communication, meeting rooms, staff training, presentation, and control within one facility. BIG AV coordinates these systems around the people responsible for daily operation and support.

We plan for multiple stakeholders, existing infrastructure, phased replacement, and clear handover. Modernization can begin with a bounded assessment and move forward without assuming every system must be replaced at once.
