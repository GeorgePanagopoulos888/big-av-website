Our markets

Entertainment and Venues

Sound, image, lighting and control, built for the audience and the people running the show.

The environment

From the first cue to the last seat.

BIG AV designs and integrates complete systems for theatres, arenas, casinos, attractions, clubs, houses of worship and live-event spaces. The work ranges from speech and performance systems to immersive environments where media, sound and lighting tell the story together.

Audience coverage, sightlines, content and operator positions guide the design. Behind the experience, we plan the distribution, control and infrastructure the venue needs for daily operation and changing productions.

Systems

The systems behind the experience.

Performance audioSound reinforcement, processing, distribution and monitoring for the audience, performers and operators.
Video and projectionDisplays, projection, switching, capture and media distribution for live presentation and recorded content.
Show and venue controlOperator interfaces, cue sequences and presets that coordinate equipment for performances, rehearsals and everyday use.
Immersive attractionsSynchronized media, audio, lighting and control across exhibits and immersive environments.
Show lightingLighting systems and programming coordinated with performance requirements, show control and the venue infrastructure.

Project delivery

Commissioned for the performance.

We carry the system from design and installation through programming, tuning and testing. Commissioning checks sound coverage, signal routes, lighting sequences and the controls operators will use during an event.

For an existing venue, upgrades can be planned around rehearsals, performances and maintenance windows. The operating team receives documentation and training for the completed systems. We provide troubleshooting and replacement planning as the venue evolves.
