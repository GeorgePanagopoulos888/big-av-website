Our markets

Retail and Hospitality

Sound, media and lighting control for spaces people come to shop, dine and stay.

The environment

The atmosphere is part of the business.

BIG AV designs and integrates AV systems for retail stores, restaurants, hotels and hospitality venues. Audio, digital media, lighting controls and event systems are planned together, from the guest-facing spaces to the controls staff use each day.

We work through how the space changes: opening, daytime service, private events and closing. For businesses with multiple locations, we develop consistent equipment and operating standards that can be adapted to each site.

Systems

For guests, staff and every location.

Zoned audio and digital mediaMusic, displays, menus and signage arranged for different spaces and service periods, with content distribution across individual or multiple sites.
Lighting and AV controlSimple staff interfaces and scheduled scenes for sound, displays and lighting, including opening, service and closing routines.
Events and private functionsFlexible microphones, presentation, audio and room controls for meetings, functions and programmed events.
Multiple locationsRepeatable designs, control programs and documentation to simplify rollouts, staff training and support across sites.

Project delivery

Ready for opening and everyday service.

We coordinate infrastructure and installation with the fit-out team, then program and test the systems against the way staff will run the space. Audio levels, media routes and operating scenes are checked before handover, with training for the team.

For established locations, upgrades can be phased around trading hours and planned closures. New sites can build on documented standards while accounting for their layout and equipment needs. Monitoring and troubleshooting support day-to-day operation.
