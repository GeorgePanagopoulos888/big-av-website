Our markets

Landscape

Outdoor sound and lighting control, planned as part of the landscape.

The environment

Part of the site from the start.

BIG AV designs and installs outdoor audio and control systems for landscaped properties, terraces, gardens and shared outdoor spaces. Sound coverage, lighting scenes and the controls for each area are planned alongside the physical site.

We coordinate with the landscape and construction teams on cable routes, equipment locations, power and maintenance access. Weather exposure, planting, hard surfaces and the distance between zones all influence the design.

Systems

Sound and control across the property.

Outdoor audio and zonesDistributed sound arranged for the intended listening areas, with independent sources, volume and schedules for spaces used at different times.
Lighting controlLighting scenes, schedules and shared controls that coordinate outdoor lighting with audio and daily use.
Site infrastructureCable pathways, network connections, power, enclosures and equipment positions suited to outdoor conditions and planned maintenance access.
Monitoring and seasonal operationEquipment status and fault reporting across the property, with startup, shutdown and maintenance plans for the people responsible for the site.

Project delivery

Tested where it will be used.

Installation is coordinated with the landscape works so infrastructure is in place before surfaces are finished. We tune audio on site, check coverage between zones and test lighting scenes, schedules and controls with the owner or operating team.

Existing installations can be extended or upgraded in phases. Equipment access and seasonal service needs remain part of the plan, alongside how the property will look and be used.
