VERTICAL MARKET

Landscape

We coordinate sound, control, and lighting-adjacent technology around weather, coverage, infrastructure, and seasonal operation.

SECTION 1 | THE ENVIRONMENTS

Coverage that belongs outdoors.

Landscape systems must work across changing weather, site conditions, and seasonal use. BIG AV plans outdoor audio, control, and lighting-adjacent systems as part of the site rather than as indoor equipment moved outside.

We coordinate pathways, zones, equipment locations, user controls, monitoring, and maintenance access. Existing systems can be assessed and modernized in phases where replacement is not the right first step.
